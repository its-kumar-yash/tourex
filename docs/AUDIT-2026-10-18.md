Notes

- LCP on the simulated slow 4G profile is the hero or cover photo. Real photos
  uploaded through the admin (WebP, 1600px wide or less) will do better than the
  Unsplash placeholders, which are re-encoded on every cold request.
- `/packages` loses 8 SEO points for "no meta description": the page is
  dynamic (filters come from the URL) and Next.js 15 streams its metadata into
  the body for browsers. Known crawlers receive it in the head.
- Unknown package slugs return a real 404; admin and account routes are
  `noindex` and redirect signed-out visitors to login.
